1. Quoting & estimates

Most contractors we sit with are pricing jobs after dinner. A phone form feeding a priced template, plus automatic follow-up, is the fastest win.

  • Measurements captured on site, once
  • Pricing rules applied automatically
  • Follow-up sent if the quote isn't opened in 48 hours
The owner stopped doing estimates at 10pm.

2. Invoicing & chasing payment

Raise the invoice when the job is marked complete, then let a polite escalation ladder do the chasing.

What to watch for

Keep a human on anything over a set amount, and on first reminders to new clients.

3. Re-typing data between systems

A single sync so nothing gets typed twice.
