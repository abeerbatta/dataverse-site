Four ways in

Most engagements start with an audit and end with something running quietly in the background. You can also hire us for one piece.

01

AI readiness audit

Two weeks inside your actual week. We map every repetitive handoff, price the time it costs you, and hand back a ranked plan — yours to keep whether or not you hire us for the build.

02

Workflow automation builds

Quotes, intake, scheduling, invoicing, follow-up. We build on the tools you already pay for, document it in plain language, and make sure someone on your team can change it without calling us.

03

Custom AI agents

An assistant that knows your business — answering after-hours enquiries, triaging email, drafting the reply you'd have written anyway. Scoped narrowly, tested against real transcripts, with a human in the loop where it matters.

04

Data cleanup & integration

Nothing automates well on top of four spreadsheets and a CRM nobody trusts. We consolidate, de-duplicate, and connect your systems so the rest of the work actually holds.
